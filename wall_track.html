How can the model stay on track?

It receives ideal lane/heading errors, speed, previous steering, four track previews, curvature, projected footprint margin and a speed-dependent steering-budget feature. Neither feature clamps its output. These are observable inputs—not an explanation of individual neuron reasoning. Blue preview guides are geometric sensors, not camera vision or learned attention.

The neural driver learns steering and target speed. Pedals/gears are automatic; no wall-avoidance shield overrides its actions.
