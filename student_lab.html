2. Build your training dataset

Use A/D or the steering controls and W/S for target speed. Capture the inputs before each action. Split entire driving episodes, not neighboring frames. Labels are your commands—not reward scores.

3. Inspect one real training step

This performs a genuine minibatch update on your student network. Adam uses gradient history: the update is not simply minus learning rate times gradient.

Load or capture examples first.

Actual minibatch inputs, predictions and errors

Steering and target-speed labels/predictions are normalized to [-1,1]. The mean of the displayed weighted per-example errors is the minibatch loss.

The displayed weights are selected by gradient magnitude. They are not a causal explanation of the car's decisions. Loss can increase on a step; the values here are measured, not scripted improvements.

4. Rules versus learning

Compare a fixed lane/heading rule, a pure-pursuit expert and your selected neural policy. Each sees matched starts. Failures freeze at their endpoint; a short failed run is not a fast lap.

5. Remove inputs and retrain

All variants start from scratch with the same architecture, examples, batch schedule and update budget. Inputs are removed during both fitting and driving—not just switched off on a pretrained model.

“No future geometry” removes all previews and explicit curvature. “No heading” removes heading channels and the derived margin, but geometric previews can still encode orientation. “No margin” retains lane/heading information from which margin can be inferred.

6. Generalization and evaluation leakage

Training uses the eleven existing training circuits. Three newly generated circuits were withheld from every clean reference model until all weights and validation-based selections were frozen. Legacy holdouts have already been seen in earlier work.

Deliberately break the rule—only as a lesson

A separate model clone can train on the very test labels it is scored against. The resulting number is explicitly contaminated and cannot support a generalization claim.

Once you reveal or reuse a test set, retraining is exploratory. Clearing examples does not erase test exposure. The seal is a classroom procedure, not cryptographic secrecy; source data is inspectable offline.

7. Robustness and distribution shift

Keep the driver and starts fixed; alter observations or the plant. Delayed inputs are genuinely old observations. Noise affects lane, heading and speed sensors; ideal previews remain unchanged.

Grip scales the lateral-acceleration limit and available braking in this toy model. It does not implement tire slip, weather or realistic F1 handling. The policy does not receive a grip measurement; the ordinary speed controller still senses current speed. Random streams are reproducible within each runtime, but browser RNG is not NumPy's RNG.
